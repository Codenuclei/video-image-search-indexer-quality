# OpenRouter for Carousel Script Quality

**Ask:** Fund OpenRouter (credits or a plan) so carousel scripts can use the right model for each job — and stay online when Gemini or Anthropic die.

---

## Why this matters

Carousel quality is the script: themes, topics, hooks, intent, and slide copy. Weak hooks lose the scroll; verbatim dumps feel unfinished; bad themes that get cached poison every hook downstream. Layout and frames cannot fix bad words.

## What OpenRouter gives back

One funded account unlocks many models, so we get:

- **Stronger hooks and titles** — polish that sounds human, not transcript-dump
- **Less filler, more intent** — clearer topics and directional lines users actually trust
- **Failover when vendors die** — Gemini 429s / Anthropic key pain no longer silently ship heuristic fallbacks
- **Quality that caches well** — better first generations stick; we stop reusing weak copy until someone regenerates

Image gen can wait; scripts are the bottleneck and the product. Vision is the other lever worth funding: better frame picks when local heuristics are not enough.

## Which models matter (and why)

**Luna / Flash Lite** — cheap volume drafts: topic trees, first-pass hooks, intent. What you get: coverage at low cost so we can try and keep the best structure.

**Claude Sonnet / Luna Pro** — polish that sounds human. What you get: theme titles and final hooks people stop for, without the verbatim feel of fallbacks.

**Vision** — score and rank carousel frame candidates beyond local heuristics. What you get: guest-centered, front-facing faces (not backs of heads), and crops that match slide intent. We already have local front-face scoring; vision upgrades judgment when Gemini/credits fail or heuristics miss.

**Default shape:** draft cheap → polish once with Sonnet/Luna Pro → vision when choosing or checking frames.

## Vision: centered, front-facing frames

Why it matters: Instagram carousels look intentional when the guest faces the camera and sits near frame center — not profiles, edge faces, or the back of a head.

What vision returns:

- **Facing camera** — prefer low yaw/pitch (front-facing) over profile or turned-away shots
- **Centered presence** — face size and position near the middle of the frame; reject tiny/edge faces
- **Better crop intent** — rank candidates so the slide reads as designed, not accidental
- **Optional hook fit** — multimodal pick of the frame that best matches a hook line (image + caption)

Outcome: slides that look deliberate on Instagram, not leftover video frames.
